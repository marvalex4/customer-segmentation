# Customer Segmentation Analysis: K-Means Clustering

## Overview
An unsupervised machine learning project using K-Means clustering 
to segment mall customers based on annual income and spending score, 
helping identify the most valuable customer groups for targeted marketing.

## Questions Explored
1. What is the distribution of customer age, income and spending score?
2. Is there a relationship between annual income and spending score?
3. What is the optimal number of customer segments?
4. What are the characteristics of each customer segment?
5. Which customer segment should the mall target most?

## Tools Used
1. Python
2. Pandas
3. NumPy
4. Matplotlib
5. Seaborn
6. Scikit-learn (KMeans, StandardScaler)
7. Jupyter Notebook

## Dataset
1. Mall_Customers.csv (sourced from Kaggle)
2. 200 mall customer records

## Model Details
1. Algorithm: K-Means Clustering
2. Optimal Clusters: 5 (determined by Elbow Method)
3. Features Used: Annual Income, Spending Score

## Customer Segments Identified
1. High Income High Spenders - Prime targets for premium marketing
2. Low Income High Spenders - Loyal and engaged customers
3. Average Income Average Spenders - Largest segment, growth potential
4. High Income Low Spenders - Untapped opportunity segment
5. Low Income Low Spenders - Budget conscious customers

## Key Insights
1. 5 distinct customer segments were identified
2. Annual income alone does not predict spending behavior
3. High Income High Spenders are the most valuable segment
4. High Income Low Spenders represent an untapped opportunity
5. Younger customers between 20-40 dominate the customer base
